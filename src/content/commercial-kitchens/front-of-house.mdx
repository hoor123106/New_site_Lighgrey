ourProcessDescription: "Front of house equipment design at Steelcraft is a collaborative process involving three parties: the client's operations team, the interior designer, and our fabrication team. The operations team defines the workflow — how orders are taken, how food moves from kitchen to service counter, how payment is processed, and how cleaning is managed during and after service. The interior designer defines the visual parameters — materials, colours, heights, and the relationship of the FOH counter to the wider interior. Steelcraft's role is to produce a fabrication that satisfies both requirements simultaneously.\n\nWe produce a detailed drawing showing every dimension, material, and equipment integration point — reviewed by all parties before fabrication begins. Installation is carried out by our team in coordination with the interior fit-out contractor, and a post-installation walkthrough confirms that every operational and aesthetic requirement has been met."
