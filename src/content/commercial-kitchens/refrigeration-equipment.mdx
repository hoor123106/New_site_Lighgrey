feature2Description: "For kitchens operating cook-chill or cook-freeze systems — including hotel banqueting operations, central production kitchens, and high-volume catering facilities — blast chillers and shock freezers are essential food safety tools, not optional extras. A blast chiller brings cooked food from +70°C to +3°C in under 90 minutes, arresting bacterial growth and extending safe shelf life. A shock freezer reaches -18°C at the food's core within the time frames required by food safety regulation. Steelcraft specifies blast chilling and shock freezing equipment based on your batch sizes and production cycles, and advises on the critical integration of this equipment into your HACCP food safety management system.
