ourProcessDescription: "Steelcraft's exhaust hood system process begins with an exhaust calculation — a formal document that establishes the required extraction volume in cubic metres per hour for each hood, based on the cooking equipment installed beneath it and the kitchen's make-up air supply capacity. This calculation is produced by our team and submitted to the MEP contractor and, where required, to the relevant UAE authority for review.\n\nHood fabrication takes place once dimensions and positions are confirmed against the approved kitchen layout drawing. Ductwork routing is agreed with the MEP contractor before fabrication — ensuring that connection spigots are positioned correctly and that duct sizes are consistent with the calculated extraction volumes. Installation of the fabricated hood is carried out by Steelcraft's team; ductwork connection and fan commissioning are coordinated with the MEP contractor. A post-commissioning airflow test confirms that the installed system achieves the calculated extraction volumes before the kitchen is handed over."
