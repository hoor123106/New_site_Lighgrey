ourProcessDescription: "Steelcraft uses a structured capacity calculation to specify warewashing equipment correctly. The process begins with peak cover count, menu type (the ratio of crockery, glassware, and cookware per cover), and the available service window between seatings. We calculate pieces per hour, translate this into baskets per hour, and select a machine whose rated capacity provides a comfortable working margin above peak demand — typically 20–30% headroom to allow for re-washing and variation. Hood-type machines are generally specified for operations processing between 40 and 100 covers per sitting; conveyor systems for larger volumes. We also review the physical dishwash area: soiled inlet, clean outlet, table heights, drainage falls, electrical supply, and ventilation — producing a complete dishwash area layout before equipment is ordered."
