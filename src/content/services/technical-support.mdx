feature2Title: "Preventive Maintenance & Service Contracts"
feature2Description: "The most reliable way to reduce breakdown frequency and extend equipment operating life is a structured preventive maintenance programme — scheduled, documented, and carried out by technicians with equipment-specific knowledge. Steelcraft offers service contracts for commercial kitchen and industrial bakery equipment that cover periodic preventive maintenance visits at intervals matched to the equipment type and usage intensity, a documented inspection and maintenance checklist completed and signed off at each visit, priority breakdown response for contracted customers, and agreed spare parts pricing for scheduled replacements.\n\nPreventive maintenance visits cover the checks and adjustments that manufacturer service schedules specify: cleaning of burner assemblies and ignition systems; inspection and replacement of door gaskets and seals; calibration of thermostats, temperature probes, and control systems; lubrication of moving parts; inspection of electrical connections and controls; and cleaning of condensers and evaporator coils on refrigeration equipment. A well-maintained piece of equipment lasts significantly longer, consumes less energy, and fails less frequently than one that receives only reactive maintenance."
