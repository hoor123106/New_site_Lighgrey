feature3Description: "Steelcraft's manufacturing process is built around a drawing-first discipline that eliminates the ambiguity and rework that results from verbal or sketch-based specifications. Every custom fabrication begins with a detailed drawing produced by our in-house draughting team — showing all external dimensions, internal configurations, material gauge, surface finish specification, connection points, and any integrated components such as sinks, drainage points, or lighting fittings.\n\nThis drawing is reviewed with the client before fabrication begins, and written approval is obtained before any material is cut. During fabrication, quality checkpoints are maintained at key stages: material receipt inspection, dimensional checking of cut and formed components, weld quality inspection, and final dimensional and surface quality inspection of the completed item. Every fabrication is checked against the approved drawing before despatch, and any item that does not meet specification is reworked or remade before it leaves the production facility — not after it arrives on site."
