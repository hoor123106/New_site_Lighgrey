feature1Title: "Strategic Layout & Workflow Design"
feature1Description: "The most expensive kitchen mistake is a design error — because it costs nothing on paper and everything in operation. Steelcraft approaches commercial kitchen and industrial bakery design as a strategic exercise, not a space-filling exercise. We begin by understanding the operation: the menu or product range, the volume of covers or production output required, the service model, the team structure, and the hours of operation. From this we develop a layout that supports a logical, efficient flow — ingredients arriving at goods-in, moving through prep and production, reaching the cooking or baking stage, and exiting through service or despatch without unnecessary cross-movement, cross-contamination risk, or congestion.\n\nEvery design decision is tested against the operational reality of a busy service or production shift — not just the appearance of the finished drawing. We consider equipment access for cleaning and maintenance, sufficient cold and dry storage relative to delivery frequency, and adequate circulation space for kitchen teams working at peak."

feature2Title: "MEP Engineering & Technical Drawings"
feature2Description: "A kitchen design is only complete when the mechanical, electrical, and plumbing requirements are fully specified alongside the equipment layout. Steelcraft provides a comprehensive technical drawing package that equips the MEP contractor, civil contractor, and fit-out team with everything they need to build and connect the kitchen without interpretation or assumption.\n\nOur design deliverables include AutoCAD equipment layout drawings with dimensions and floor fixings; MEP engineering drawings showing all service connection points for gas, water, drainage, and electrical supply; electro-mechanical schedules listing every piece of equipment with its utility requirements, connection specifications, and heat dissipation load; exhaust and fresh air calculations determining the duct sizes, fan capacities, and make-up air volumes required; and heat load calculations to inform HVAC system sizing. These deliverables are produced to the standard required by UAE municipal authorities, TRAKHEES, and Dubai Civil Defence where applicable."

feature3Title: "HACCP Workflow & Food Safety Integration"
feature3Description: "Food safety is not a compliance exercise to be addressed after the kitchen is designed — it is a design constraint that must be built into the layout from the outset. Steelcraft's design process incorporates HACCP principles at every stage, ensuring that the physical layout of the kitchen supports the food safety management system the operator will be required to implement and document.\n\nThis means designing clear physical or procedural separation between raw and cooked food zones; ensuring adequate hand-washing facilities are positioned at the points where they will actually be used; locating refrigeration equipment to minimise the distance that temperature-sensitive ingredients travel; positioning dishwashing and waste handling areas to prevent cross-contamination of clean surfaces; and ensuring that cleaning access to all equipment surfaces is practical within the constraints of the operational layout. Where Dubai Municipality, Abu Dhabi Food Control Authority, or Sharjah Municipality inspections are anticipated, Steelcraft's design team is familiar with the specific requirements of each authority."
