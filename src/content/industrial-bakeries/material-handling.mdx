feature2Title: "Pneumatic & Mechanical Conveying Systems"
feature2Description: "Moving ingredients from storage to production efficiently — without spillage, contamination, or manual handling — requires the right conveying technology for each material. Steelcraft specifies pneumatic conveying systems for flour and fine powders, mechanical screw conveyors for granular ingredients, belt and bucket elevators for bulk grains and seeds, and vibratory feeders for precise low-volume ingredient delivery to mixing and dosing stations.\n\nConveying system design considers material characteristics (particle size, density, flowability, moisture sensitivity), the distance and elevation change between storage and point of use, and the required transfer rate in kilograms per hour. All conveying systems are designed for easy cleaning and access, with sanitary construction standards throughout. We coordinate conveying system layout with the wider bakery floor plan to eliminate cross-traffic and minimise product loss."

feature3Title: "Ingredient Dosing & Recipe Management"
feature3Description: "Precise, repeatable ingredient dosing is one of the most significant contributors to product consistency in industrial baking. Steelcraft supplies automatic dosing systems for liquid ingredients — water, oils, improvers, and liquid sugars — using flow meters and volumetric dosing pumps calibrated to recipe specifications. Dry ingredient dosing systems use loss-in-weight scales and automatic dispensing valves to ensure each batch receives the correct quantity of every ingredient within defined tolerance.\n\nFor bakeries managing multiple recipes across multiple production lines, integrated recipe management software links dosing equipment to a central control system — allowing recipe changes to be executed at the control panel without manual re-measurement. This eliminates human weighing error, reduces ingredient waste, and provides a complete digital record of every batch produced."
