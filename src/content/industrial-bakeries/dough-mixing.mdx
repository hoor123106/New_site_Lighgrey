feature1Description: "Dough mixing is the most technically critical step in the baking process — the wrong mixer, the wrong speed, or the wrong mixing time will compromise every batch, regardless of ingredient quality. Steelcraft supplies the full range of batch mixing technology to match every dough type and production volume. Spiral mixers are the standard choice for most bread and roll doughs, delivering efficient gluten network development with low friction heat transfer — preserving dough temperature and fermentation integrity. Fork mixers are preferred for highly hydrated sourdoughs and artisan-style doughs where gentle handling is essential. Planetary mixers provide versatility across dough, cream, and batter applications for patisserie and mixed-production bakeries.\n\nAll mixers are available across a wide range of bowl capacities — from 20-litre bench-top units through to 600-litre floor-standing production mixers."

feature2Title: "Horizontal Mixers & Continuous Mixing Systems"
feature2Description: "For high-volume industrial bakeries producing bread, buns, and rolls at scale, batch mixing may represent a production bottleneck. Horizontal mixers offer significantly larger batch capacities than vertical spiral or fork designs, with twin-arm or sigma blade configurations suited to stiff doughs, rye doughs, and specialty grain products. For the highest production volumes — typically exceeding several tonnes of dough per hour — continuous mixing systems eliminate the batch cycle entirely, delivering a consistent stream of homogeneous dough to downstream dividers and moulders without interruption.\n\nSteelcraft specifies horizontal and continuous mixing systems based on required dough output in kilograms per hour, dough temperature management requirements, and integration with upstream ingredient dosing and downstream dough make-up equipment. All systems are supplied with appropriate motor protection, control panels, and interlocking safety systems."

feature3Title: "Carousel & Vacuum Mixing Systems"
feature3Description: "For bakeries operating multiple spiral or fork mixers serving a single production line, carousel mixing systems provide a practical solution to throughput maximisation — a rotating platform carries multiple mixer bowls through filling, mixing, and discharge positions in a continuous cycle, maximising mixer utilisation without requiring additional floor area. This approach is particularly effective for high-diversity production environments where many different doughs are mixed in overlapping schedules.\n\nVacuum and pressure mixing technology offers additional control for specialist applications: vacuum mixing removes entrapped air to produce a denser, more uniform crumb structure, while pressure mixing accelerates gluten development and reduces mixing time. Steelcraft supplies vacuum mixing equipment for producers of sandwich bread, burger buns, and other products where tight crumb structure and uniform slice appearance are commercial requirements."
