section3Title: "Automatic Stacking & Packaging Integration"
section3Description: "Flatbread production at volume requires packaging to keep pace with production output — a mismatch between baking speed and packaging capacity creates product accumulation, quality deterioration, and line stoppages. Steelcraft designs automatic stacking and packaging systems as an integral component of every flatbread production line, not as an afterthought.\n\nAutomatic stacking systems count and stack pita and flatbread into defined pack quantities — typically 4, 6, 8, or 10 pieces — and feed these stacks into the packaging machine infeed without manual handling. Flow wrap and thermoform packaging machines seal each stack in the appropriate food-grade film, with date coding and batch marking applied inline. Steelcraft coordinates line speeds across the production, stacking, and packaging sections to ensure a balanced, continuous production flow from oven to finished packaged product."
