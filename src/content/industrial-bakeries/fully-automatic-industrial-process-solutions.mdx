At the dough make-up stage, automatic systems integrate ingredient dosing, continuous or batch mixing, and dough transfer to make-up equipment without manual intervention.\n\nDough make-up lines for bread, buns, rolls, and flatbreads use automatic dividers, rounders, intermediate conveyors, moulders, and tray or tin loaders, with each unit operating at the line speed determined by the system's master control. section2Title: "Automated Proofing, Baking & Cooling"
section2Description: "The proofing, baking, and cooling stages of an automated industrial line must operate as a precisely balanced sequence — the output rate of the proofer must match the input rate of the oven, and the output rate of the oven must match the input rate of the cooling system. Any imbalance creates accumulation at one stage or starvation at another, reducing effective throughput and creating product quality problems.\n\nSteelcraft designs automated proofing conveyors and tunnel proofers with adjustable belt speeds and climate control systems that maintain target fermentation conditions at the required throughput rate. Tunnel ovens are specified with the precise combination of zone temperatures, conveyor speed, and steam injection that produces the target bake profile at the line's operating speed. Post-bake cooling spirals and ambient cooling conveyors bring product to the packaging-safe temperature within the available floor area without forced cooling that damages crust or crumb structure."
