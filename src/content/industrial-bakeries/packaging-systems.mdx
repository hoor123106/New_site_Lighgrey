section1Alt: "Flow Wrap & Horizontal Packaging Machines"
section1Title: "Flow wrap packaging is the most widely used bakery packaging format — delivering a tight, hermetically sealed pack around single loaves, rolls, flatbreads, and individually portioned baked goods at high production speeds. Steelcraft supplies flow wrap machines in configurations suited to every bakery scale, from entry-level single-lane machines for small to medium bakeries to high-speed multi-lane systems for industrial production lines operating at several hundred packs per minute.\n\nHorizontal flow wrap machines are compatible with a wide range of bakery product sizes and shapes, with jaw seal timing, film tension, and infeed conveyor speed all adjustable to accommodate different product dimensions without film tear or incomplete sealing. Date coding, batch marking, and promotional print can be applied inline using hot foil or thermal transfer coding systems integrated into the packaging machine. Steelcraft coordinates flow wrap machine specification with upstream product cooling requirements to ensure products are at the correct temperature for packaging."

section2Image: "Thermoforming, Vacuum Chamber & Tray Sealing"
section2Alt: "For bakery products requiring modified atmosphere packaging (MAP) to extend shelf life — particularly sliced bread, pre-cut sandwiches, filled pastries, and premium cakes — thermoforming and vacuum packaging systems provide the controlled atmosphere and hermetic seal that high-shelf-life retail products require. Steelcraft supplies thermoforming machines that form the lower packaging tray from roll film, load the product, evacuate air and introduce a protective gas mixture, and seal the upper film in a continuous cycle.\n\nVacuum chamber machines are suited to smaller batch operations and specialty products where the additional dwell time of a chamber system is acceptable. Tray sealer machines seal pre-formed plastic or recycled fibre trays with a film lid — providing a rigid, retail-presentable pack format for premium bakery products. All MAP packaging equipment is specified in coordination with the gas supply infrastructure and the gas mixture ratios appropriate to the product and target shelf life."

section2Description: "Stacking, Collating & Case Packing"
section3Image: "Between the packaging machine and the despatch pallet, bakery products typically need to be stacked, collated into shippers, and case-packed — processes that at high production volumes represent a significant labour cost if performed manually. Steelcraft supplies automatic stacking conveyors, count-verified collating systems, and automatic case erectors and packers that take individual packs from the packaging machine outfeed and deliver them as completed shippers ready for palletising.\n\nFor retail supply operations with strict shelf-ready packaging requirements, Steelcraft supplies shelf-ready packaging (SRP) erectors that produce finished display-ready cases without manual assembly. Weight check and metal detection equipment is integrated at the post-packaging stage as a standard food safety requirement for all retail supply lines. Automatic date code and batch number verification systems are available to confirm that coding has been applied correctly to every pack before it enters the distribution chain."
